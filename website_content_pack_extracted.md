Boiler repair

We diagnose boiler and heating-system faults and explain the available repair options before work proceeds. Typical issues include loss of heating or hot water, pressure problems, leaks, unusual noises, control faults and unreliable performance.

Boiler installation and replacement

6. Compliance Services Page

SEO title

EPC & Electrical Safety Certificates | Cystem Green Solutions

Hero

Clear Property Compliance for Owners, Landlords and Agents

We make essential property assessments easier to arrange and understand. Our compliance services help clients prepare properties for letting, sale, management and improvement.

Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR)

An EICR is a formal inspection and test of a property’s fixed electrical installation. It identifies damage, deterioration, defects and conditions that may present danger or require improvement. Following the inspection, the client receives a written report setting out the findings and any classification codes.

Landlord electrical-safety inspections

Domestic EICRs for homeowners and buyers

Portfolio and block booking support

Clear explanation of observations and classification codes

Quotation for remedial work where required

Customer-facing label: Electrical Safety Certificate (EICR). Use the formal term throughout the detailed page because an EICR is a condition report rather than a generic compliance certificate.

Energy Performance Certificate (EPC)

We assess the energy performance of domestic property and lodge the resulting certificate on the official register. The report includes the current rating, potential rating and recommended energy-efficiency measures.
